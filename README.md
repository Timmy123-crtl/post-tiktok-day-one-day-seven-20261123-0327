Ghost Dash, a tiny agent-built browser game, on the day it was made and after a week of daily updates — the short video for make.fun's "Day one, day seven" bounty. Day one: one star at a time, one slow chaser. Day seven: the same map and the same start, with five stars, coins, a shield power-up, three chasers, two patrollers, combos and levels. The video cuts between the identical moments a week apart, the camera never moving, and closes on the newer build being played, then make.fun's referral end card.

Play the games: open `game-v1/index.html` (day one) or `game-v7/index.html` (day seven) in a browser. Steer with the autoplay built in (`?auto=1`), or add your own keys.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
